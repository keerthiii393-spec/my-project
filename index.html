Room settings (AC / seats) and data notes

The PDFs list which room each class uses but not AC or capacity, so tick AC rooms and enter seats here (saved in your browser); the AI finder and AC filter use it. Floors are inferred from room numbers (1xx = Ground, 2xx = First … 7xx = Sixth; IST 20/21 workshop = Ground). Cells without a room number (Che lab, Yoga, some PPS lab slots) are not shown. Year I uses the 9-period bell (9:00–17:05); Year II the 9:00–16:50 bell.
